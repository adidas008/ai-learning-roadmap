# AI-901 Study Plan & AI Engineer Roadmap

## 1. AI-901 Exam Objectives
* **Domain 1**: Identify AI Concepts and Capabilities (40–45%)
* **Domain 2**: Implement AI Solutions using Microsoft Foundry (55–60%)

## 2. Study Schedule & Milestones
* **Phase 1 (Days 1–3)**: Master Responsible AI 6 Principles & Azure AI Vision/Language Services.
* **Phase 2 (Days 4–6)**: Study Microsoft Foundry Portal, Prompt Engineering, & Python SDK integration.
* **Phase 3 (Days 7–10)**: Complete Practice Assessments on Microsoft Learn & Mock Exams.

## 3. AI Engineer Skill Growth Roadmap
1. **Foundations**: Azure AI Services (AI-901) & Basic Python SDKs.
2. **Intermediate**: Prompt Engineering, Embeddings, & Vector Databases (Azure AI Search).
3. **Advanced**: RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) Architecture & Agentic Workflows.
